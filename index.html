Ninety's Studios Publisher

Ninety's Studios Publisher is a private publishing tool used by Ninety's Studios to manage its own YouTube channel, youtube.com/@ninetysstudios.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules Ninety's Studios' own YouTube Shorts to the Ninety's Studios channel.
  • Checks that each upload was published correctly, and that the same video is never uploaded twice.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports so Ninety's Studios can see how its videos perform.

The app is used only by the owner of the Ninety's Studios channel. It is not offered to the public, and it doesn't access any other person's YouTube account or data.

It uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
